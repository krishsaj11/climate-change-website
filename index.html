Understanding Our Changing Planet

WHAT IS CLIMATE CHANGE?

Climate change refers to long-term changes in the Earth's temperature and weather patterns. These changes can happen naturally, but human activities such as burning fossil fuels and deforestation have greatly increased the rate of climate change. It can affect ecosystems, wildlife, sea levels, and the lives of people around the world.

WHAT ARE THE CAUSES OF CLIMATE CHANGE?

1. Burning Fossil Fuels

Coal, oil, and natural gas are burned for electricity, transportation, and industry. This releases greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide.

2. Deforestation

Trees absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. When forests are cut down, less carbon dioxide is removed from the air, and some of the stored carbon is released.

3. Agriculture and Livestock

Farming and livestock can produce greenhouse gases such as methane and nitrous oxide, which contribute to global warming.

EFFECTS OF CLIMATE CHANGE

1. Rising Temperatures

Climate change is causing Earth's average temperature to rise. Higher temperatures can lead to more frequent and intense heatwaves.

2. Melting Ice and Rising Sea Levels

Higher temperatures cause glaciers and ice sheets to melt. This contributes to rising sea levels, which can threaten coastal communities and ecosystems.

3. Extreme Weather

Climate change can influence weather patterns and increase the risk or intensity of events such as heatwaves, heavy rainfall, droughts, and some storms.
